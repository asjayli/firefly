@design 01
# Anchors for the repair skill (runtime-facing, design 01).
+trigger: repair missing or drifted firefly-managed assets
+mandate: Preserve backups before replacement
+input: The doctor report, when one is at hand
+output: What was repaired, what was backed up
+verdict-or-state: Repair `MISS` and `DRIFT` states from firefly-owned copies
+handoff: The reply returns to the requester
+stop: upstream OpenSpec version differs from the override snapshot version
-: APPROVE
-: deletes a drifted asset
-: repairs without a backup
