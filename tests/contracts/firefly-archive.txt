@design 25,22
# firefly-archive: the three separately commissioned endpoints — readiness
# (read-only), local archive, launch — their non-authorization of each other,
# the closeout ledger, the snapshot-ref cleanup, and the stops.

# trigger: the three endpoints each need their own commission; acceptance,
# a finished sync or a readiness conclusion opens none of the others.
+trigger: Three separately commissionable endpoints
+trigger: A readiness assessment
+trigger: the local archive of one named change
+trigger: a launch under its own mandate
+trigger: Commissioned separately after `ACCEPT`
+trigger: The handoff is a route, not a commission
+trigger: No separate commission

# mandate: archive covers the sync and the local move only; no traffic action
# from readiness or archive; the authorized writes are not a candidate change.
+mandate: it never covers a push, a release or a rewrite of shared history
+mandate: covers no traffic action and no launch
+mandate: is still the accepted candidate
+mandate: the local archive move of the change directory
+mandate: A surprise gate
+mandate: a uniform rollout percentage is never copied over them

# input: a change named by the commission, never from context; reservations of
# the incrementally accepted candidate only; the drill record on the same
# candidate; thresholds from service objectives and baselines.
+input: A change is never picked from conversation context
+input: taken item by item from the reservation list
+input: the drill record must name the same candidate
+input: the service objectives and the baselines
+input: When an observation window spans a complete business cycle, its end and its successor are written to the task record

# output: the ledger in the reply with no standalone file; the inventory, the
# migration decision and the rollout plan on their carriers; refs dropped.
+output: no standalone file
+output: `openspec/changes/<id>/archive-inventory.md`
+output: `openspec/changes/<id>/deprecation.md`
+output: `.superpowers/sdd/progress.md`
+output: Preserve `refs/firefly/snapshots/`
+output: is never recorded as migrated
+output: Zero traffic before a complete business cycle has ended is not a basis for removal

# verdict-or-state: ready, release authorized and live are three different
# facts; touches-required fails the assessment; the state exits of a launch.
+verdict-or-state: three different facts
+verdict-or-state: marked as touching required behavior fails the readiness assessment
+verdict-or-state: not launch-ready
+verdict-or-state: live and verified
+verdict-or-state: returns it to `accepted`
+verdict-or-state: returns it to `in-progress`
+verdict-or-state: Until the return has arrived and been checked, the item stays open

# handoff: the after-ACCEPT route; the comparison reviewed before anything is
# applied; the drill record from firefly-trace; no new write authority back.
+handoff: What may follow is commissioned separately
+handoff: before anything is applied
+handoff: The drill record, handed to the release owner
+handoff: brings no new write authority
+handoff: none of them alone establishes that nobody depends on the old entry

# stop: the unresolved change, the missing commission, semantic loss outside
# the envelope, validation outside the envelope, a launch beyond its plan.
+stop: the current conclusion, the missing evidence or authority, and the responsible owner
+stop: already archived
+stop: Semantic loss
+stop: inside the archive authorization envelope
+stop: escalates immediately
+stop: Owner: the requester

# forbidden: the retired verdict word; the retired review protocol of the old
# archive skill; the classic overreaches — expanding traffic past a stop
# threshold, pushing the archived change, inventing gates at the end.
-: APPROVE
-: Independent review contract
-: at most two new independent review agents
-: subagent-driven-development
-: expanding traffic after a stop threshold
-: pushes the archived change
-: invents acceptance criteria at the end
