@design 19

# Entry. firefly-perf is named directly, dispatched by firefly-review for the
# affected checks of the budget axis, or named by a scenario map row as the
# verifier of a measurement to interpret. The non-trigger condition is the
# missing clock or load.
+trigger: to measure a slow path, or to compare candidates against a baseline
+trigger: The mandate record must cover the writes the work takes
+trigger: the affected checks of that axis
+trigger: Only those checks run, and their results return to that review
+trigger: a measurement to interpret
+trigger: No reproducible load, or a user-waiting time that cannot be defined

# Authorization boundary. Perf measures; it does not tune. The verdict and the
# acceptable change stay with the reviewer and the requirement owner; the
# constraint entry and the CI mount are other skills' writes; a review dispatch
# leaves one write only.
+mandate: The edits that make the code faster belong to `firefly-implement`
+mandate: Redefining a criterion, a measurement method or a baseline so that a candidate passes
+mandate: These are the requirement owner's decisions; this skill prepares and presents them
+mandate: The verdict is the independent reviewer's to issue, never this skill's
+mandate: that is firefly-bounds' write
+mandate: that is firefly-guard's write, under its own authority
+mandate: The observation row and its evidence are the only write
+mandate: The handover carries no write authority

# Inputs: the interval the requester means, the constraint entry's method, the
# scenario map rows, the fixed candidate.
+input: the interval of the wait the requester means
+input: the criterion, the measurement method, the baseline source and the consequence of failure
+input: the rows of `## Scenario map` in `openspec/changes/<id>/tasks.md` that name them
+input: `firefly snapshot` fixes the candidate before the first measurement record is written

# Outputs: the measurement record per measured version with its attempts,
# configurations, samples and withdrawal reasons; the observation row through
# the recorder.
+output: one directory per measured version, never overwritten
+output: --observer verifier:firefly-perf
+output: `--note` is never omitted
+output: The recorder fills in the candidate snapshot and the spec version; nobody reports them by hand
+output: the configurations, the samples and the reason each was withdrawn

# Verdicts and states: comparable conditions, no cherry-picking, noise, lapse,
# the ballpark rule, the four statuses.
+verdict-or-state: Two numbers measured under different conditions are not compared directly
+verdict-or-state: not the best run
+verdict-or-state: is an unproven improvement
+verdict-or-state: A result made under another criterion or measurement method has lapsed (contract, Lapse)
+verdict-or-state: deliverable only when the user explicitly asked for an order of magnitude
+verdict-or-state: the result (`pass`, `fail` or `unverified`)

# Handoffs: back to the review, to firefly-bounds without write authority, to
# firefly-guard behind the owner's consent, to firefly-debug for the clock.
+handoff: its baseline, its tolerance and its owner
+handoff: writing the constraint entry needs a mandate whose permitted effects cover the spec package
+handoff: a measurement whose method is stable and whose failures have an owner
+handoff: Guard mounts it under its own write authority
+handoff: the distinguishing feedback is taken through firefly-debug first

# Stops and escalation.
+stop: `ESCALATE` with the current conclusion, the missing evidence or authority, and the responsible owner
+stop: give the conclusion in the reply only, write nothing
+stop: Record the row `unverified` with the reason and return it; the review adjudicates `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` from it
+stop: Noise is not a stop

# Forbidden. The last three are the overreaches this skill exists to prevent:
# tuning the candidate to make the number, cherry-picking the best run, and
# writing the constraint from here.
-: APPROVE
-: subagent-driven-development
-: optimizes the candidate to make the number pass
-: reports the best run as the result
-: writes the constraint entry itself
